- A decision that waits, on the owner, on another session or on evidence not yet
  measured, is a work item on the Brigade Board tagged `decision` and `cavalry`, and
  no ADR file is Proposed any more: an ADR lands Accepted with the change it
  explains, and names the work item it was carried by or made in. `just lint-docs`
  refuses a file whose Status is Proposed and says what to do, and `/next-task` reads
  the board for the decisions that wait
  ([ADR 0119](docs/decisions/0119-keep-a-decision-that-waits-on-the-brigade-board-and-never-as-a-proposed-file.md);
