# NNNN. Short title in the imperative ("Use AF_XDP for kernel bypass")

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Invariants affected:** list the `CLAUDE.md` invariant numbers this touches
- **Supersedes:** NNNN, or none

## Context

What situation forces a decision. State the constraints as facts with sources or
measurements. Two or three paragraphs at most.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one paragraph, in the present tense. The reader should be able
to stop here.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what it was, why it lost. An alternative that was never
seriously considered does not belong here.

## Evidence

Measurements, histograms, prototypes, links to the conformance or benchmark runs
that informed the choice. Numbers carry units, percentiles and the hardware they
were taken on.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to maintain, and what
would make us revisit this decision.

## References

Links to the PR, the spec section, the docs pages, and any outside discussion.
